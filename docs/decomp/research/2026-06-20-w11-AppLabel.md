# W11 DISCOVER dossier — "AppLabel" @ [0x825BB090, 0x825BB5B8)

**Verdict: REFUTED (as AppLabel) → the real owner is ViewSetting; redirected to NEEDS_DEEPER for ViewSetting.**
**Date: 2026-06-20. Mode: DISCOVER/PLANNER, read-only in main @9159.**

## TL;DR

The TU assigned was "AppLabel" with the claim that fn_825BB090 is a
`Symbol(gNull...) ctor` and the [0x825BB090, 0x825BB5B8) range is the AppLabel
class body, worth ~+12 by porting AppLabel.cpp from rb3-Wii.

**Ground-truth refutes this.** The 21 functions in that range are NOT AppLabel.
They are virtual methods of a **retail-only ViewSetting subclass** (a
`music_library_upsell` toggle setting) that does not exist in the rb3-Wii oracle.
The range is the **lower fragment of the ViewSetting.cpp .text cluster** — the
ViewSetting TU is already wired and pinned to [0x825BB5B8, 0x825BD5F0), and this
sliver sits just below that pin, squatting in the auto-blob
`auto_03_825BB090_text`.

AppLabel's body is not in this range at all. AppLabel.cpp (rb3-Wii) compiles to
121 functions / ~6.5KB of asm; this range is 0x528 bytes / 21 tiny functions.

## Evidence

### 1. Coordinates & neighbours (confirmed, splits.txt)
- Lower neighbour: `SongStatusMgr.cpp` .text ends at **0x825BB090** (splits.txt:1360).
- The sliver [0x825BB090, 0x825BB5B8) (0x528 bytes, 21 fns @0%) is owned by the
  auto-blob `default/auto_03_825BB090_text` in report.json.
- Upper neighbour: `ViewSetting.cpp` .text pinned **[0x825BB5B8, 0x825BD5F0)**
  (splits.txt:2525). ViewSetting is wired NonMatching (objects.json:728), source

So [0x825BB090, 0x825BB5B8) is bounded exactly: SongStatusMgr ends at its lo,
ViewSetting's pin starts at its hi. Zero overlap with either neighbour.

### 2. The functions are ViewSetting virtuals, not AppLabel (strings + vtables)

Strings resolved from `auto_00_82000400_rdata.obj` (base 0x82000400):

- `0x820B13DC` = `"music_library_upsell_off"`

Ghidra decompiles (read-only):

- `fn_825BB320` is an MSVC magic-static: guard bits in `DAT_82dce404 & 1/&2`

  `GetCurrentStatus()`/status override on a music-library-upsell ViewSetting.
- `fn_825BB220`/`fn_825BB238`, `fn_825BB2B4`/`...2D4`/`...2F4`: byte-jump-table

### 3. The owning subclass is absent from the rb3-Wii oracle

- rb3-Wii `ViewSetting.h` has exactly 6 classes: ViewSetting, HeaderViewSetting,
  SortViewSetting, ScoreTypeViewSetting, FilterViewSetting, ViewSettingsProvider.
  **No `music_library_upsell` subclass.**
- `grep -rn upsell ../rb3/src` hits only `utl/Symbols*` (the interned string
  tables), never a ViewSetting subclass body.
- DC3 has no `music_library_upsell` either.

Retail RB3's ViewSetting.cpp therefore contains an extra subclass that the
authoritative game oracle does not — a retail-vs-dev divergence. The bodies for
this sliver cannot be ported from the oracle; they would have to be
reconstructed from disassembly.

### 4. AppLabel is genuinely elsewhere / inlined
AppLabel.cpp exists in rb3-Wii (25KB, 121 fns) but is UNWIRED here (no
objects.json entry, no src/band3 .cpp). Its body is not in [0x825BB090,
0x825BB5B8). Most AppLabel `SetX` methods are tiny `SetDisplayText(...)` wrappers
that retail `/O1 /Ob2` likely inlined into their callers, or the cluster lives at
a different address. Locating AppLabel's real cluster is a separate fingerprint
task (not this range).

## Why this is NOT a self-contained landable item this wave

1. **Wrong TU name.** Pinning these as "AppLabel" would mis-attribute ViewSetting
   code and poison the symbol map.
2. **No oracle body.** The sliver = retail-only `music_library_upsell` ViewSetting
   subclass; rb3-Wii (authoritative) lacks it. Cannot port-then-pin from oracle.
3. **Cross-lane TU conflict.** The correct owner, ViewSetting.cpp, is ALREADY
   wired (22/70, NonMatching) and an active deepening target. Extending its pin
   downward to 0x825BB090 belongs to a ViewSetting lane, not an "AppLabel" lane —
   it would collide with any concurrent ViewSetting work (W11 rule 4: one TU per
   lane, no overlap).
4. **Honesty-gate risk of a blind pin extension.** If one extended ViewSetting's
   pin to [0x825BB090, 0x825BD5F0) WITHOUT first porting the upsell subclass, our
   compiled obj does not emit those functions, so the ~21 sliver fns would read
   0% — a ≥8-contiguous FOREIGN fn_@0% run = HONESTY-GATE violation, net ~0 or
   negative attribution. Not landable.

## The real (deferred) lever — for a ViewSetting lane, not this one
