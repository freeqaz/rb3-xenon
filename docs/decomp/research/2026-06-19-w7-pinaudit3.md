`[0x82433730..0x82439D8C)` (RndPart template + early methods). The compiled

farm (AccomplishmentProgress/Accomplishment retail stubs).

**Key distinction from the w6 "blocked" verdict:** The w6 dossier ruled Part
blocked because extending the *current pin* to `0x8243CAF0` would cross the
34-entry Accomplishment stub farm (honesty gate fail). The **dual-range approach**
adds a *separate* `.text` range that jumps over the stub farm entirely, exactly
as PropKeys already does with its two `.text` ranges.
