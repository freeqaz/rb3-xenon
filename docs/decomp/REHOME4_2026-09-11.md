## 4. Item 2 — `0x823a4fa8` **IS** a function (W3-B refuted)

W3-B dissolved L5's handoff with "it is not a function at all": not a `.pdata`
BeginAddress, inside a 264-byte `.pdata`-uncovered gap, opening
`lfs/lfs/fsubs` with no prologue. **Every observation is correct; the
conclusion is wrong**, and this repo's own CLAUDE.md says why — per lane
AUDIT-NC an 8-byte leaf stub "touches neither the stack nor LR, so it gets no
unwind record", and **7,472 of 28,956 named map rows (25.8%) are not `.pdata`
BeginAddresses**. Absence of `.pdata` is what a stackless leaf *looks like*.

The body settles it: `0x823A4FA8..0x823A50AC` is 260 B of pure FP math with
**three** return paths (`blr` @`0x823A5068`, `bnelr cr6` @`0x823A5070`,
`blr` @`0x823A50A8`), no call, no frame, no LR save; `0x823A50AC` is 4 B of
`00000000` alignment before `fn_823A50B0`. The one interior label
`.L_823A506C` is the local target of `blt cr6` @`0x823A4FE4`, not a second
entry. **One leaf function.**

Two further corrections to the record:

* **L5's "260 B" was right**, not an artifact of the carve — 260 B is the true
  body and `report.json` bills it as 260. `symbols.txt`'s `size:0x108` = 264
  is 260 + the 4 B of padding, so the *carve* over-reaches by 4 bytes.
* **The row was never a phantom.** It read `mpn 0.231`, i.e. it was *paired*
  the whole time, against our real `ObjPtrList<CharCollide>::Link`.

**Identified as `CharCollide::GetRadius(const Vector3&, Vector3&) const`**,
matching dc3's inline in `system/char/CharCollide.h` branch for branch:
`Subtract(pos, unk20c, out)` = the three `lfs/fsubs/stfs` triples; `mShape` at
`+0xb4` tested `cmpwi 3` = `>= kCollideCigar`; the two `fsel` pairs = `Clamp`;
the `fmadds` chain = `ScaleAdd`; the tail = `Interp`; the `cmpwi 0` arm =
`kCollidePlane`'s `Dot` + `Scale`. Callee channel: exactly **one** `bl`
caller, `CharHair::SimulateInternal` (`0x823a5898`), and our
`CharHair.cpp:319` calls `col->GetRadius(pt.pos, v164)` at that site.

Mangled name read out of **`CharHair.obj`'s own symbol table**, which also
*defines* it — so this is the repair-a-wrong-name class CLAUDE.md records as
paying, not a naming bet. No installed alias depends on the old name
(`symbol_aliases.json`: 0 occurrences; the 4 hits are in the **ungated**
`icf_alias_groups.json`, which the build does not consult).

Measured Δ0 / Δ0 with **Δfuzzy +0.002529pp**; `ab_measure`'s own control:
`[control none] FLAT: none UNMOVED and default not up — consistent with a pure
RE-name`. (I had pre-registered the ALIAS_SUSPECT label; the Δ prediction was
right, the *label* prediction was wrong.)

★ **The row went fuzzy 0 → 98.492, mpn 0.231 → 99.954**, and

## 6. Item 3 — `fn_8268E3F8 size:0xC` is a **jeff-side** defect, 158 sites

The `$R4` vtordisp thunk shape here is 8 instructions / 0x20 B
(`lwz -0x4(r3)` · `subf` · `lwz -0x24(r3)` · `mr r12,r11` · `lwz 0x8(r12)` ·
`add` · `addi r3,r3,0x74` · `b`). `fn_8268E3F8` is carved at **0xC**, its 4th
instruction `mr r12, r11` (`0x8268E404`) is left **inside no symbol at all**,
and a spurious `fn_8268E408 size:0x10` picks up the tail — while its three
structurally identical siblings `fn_8268E3B8`, `fn_8268E3D8`, `fn_8268E428`
and `fn_8268E448` all carve cleanly at 0x20.

**The prize is bigger than briefed: +1 fn / +32 B, not +12.** `IsNullUser`'s
own `$R4` twins `?SyncSave@BandUser@@$R4BI@7…` and
`?SyncSave@BandUser@@$R4CE@7…` are **32 B at fuzzy 100**; `IsNullUser` is
12 B at 0. The carve is the whole cause.

**The lever is jeff, not a `.text` pin and not a `symbols.txt` edit:**

* A `.text` pin defines *block* extents, not *symbol* extents. `0x8268E3F8`
  sits mid-block in `BandUser.cpp`'s `0x8268D8F8-0x8268EFD0`, and no pin can
  either delete the spurious `fn_8268E408` or extend `fn_8268E3F8`.
* `symbols.txt` is simultaneously a discovered *input* and an *output* of the
  split (the committed fixed point), so a hand-edit is re-litigated by every
  future split — and `ab_measure` **refuses** `symbols.txt` patches outright,
  so such an edit is unmeasurable through the sanctioned instrument.
* jeff has no `$R4` awareness at all: its only thunk logic
  (`src/analysis/pass.rs`) is for the CRT `__savegprlr` family.

**Sized for whoever takes it: 158 truncation sites binary-wide** where dtk
ends a symbol while real code follows outside any symbol (censused by walking
every `.s` between `.endfn` and the next `.fn` and classifying the material as
`.4byte 0x00000000` padding vs a real instruction). Opcode histogram of the
stranded instruction: `lwz` 30, `stw` 21, `addi` 11, `lfs` 7, `li` 7, `blr` 7.
`fn_8268E3F8` is one of the 158.

⚠ **Two of my own cheaper censuses for this were wrong and are recorded so
nobody repeats them.** "size:0xC followed by a 4-byte hole" yields **1,985**
rows — but almost all are *correct*: a genuine 3-instruction `$4` thunk plus
4 B of alignment padding (`fn_8268E418` is exactly that, correctly carved).
Tightening it to "…and the next symbol is size:0x10" yields **287**, still
wrong: `fn_82270288` is a complete, correct `mr`/`li`/`b` thunk whose
successor merely happens to be 0x10. **Only inspecting the hole's contents
discriminates** — the gap histogram (4 B × 17,047, 8 B × 4,931, 12 B × 4,102)
is dominated by ordinary padding, so any census keyed on gap *size* measures
padding, not defects.

I did **not** rebuild jeff (`cargo build --release` there overwrites the live
fleet binary) and did not edit `symbols.txt`.
