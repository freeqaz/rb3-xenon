## 8. ★★ The most important finding for the next milestone

The two framings of a skinned mesh **disagree**, and the disagreement is
*exactly the character's placement*.

`SceneBounds` frames via `meshWorld · bindVert` (`main_render.cpp:498`); the
pose oracle skins via the bone palette. X4c's note (`main_render.cpp:545-560`)
states the two agree **at bind pose and only there**, because that *is* the
palette invariant: `skin_b := mOffset_b · boneWorld_b == meshWorld` for every
`b`. No clip is applied, so we are at bind pose and they must agree.

Measured per mesh, player2 at character world `(−10.026, 31.389, 13.218)`:

| population | `meshWorld.v` | palette-vs-meshWorld dev |
|---|---|---|
| every **worn** mesh — `hands_naked`, `malewrist_*`, `head`, hair, eyes, eyebrows | **= the character's venue position** | **31.39** = exactly the y of that placement |
| every **unworn** catalogue mesh (`bone slots: 0` — `male/female_tattoo_*`, `*_placement_*`) | identity | **0** |

★★ **Confirmed across two venues whose placements differ by hundreds of units.**
`arena_01` (`x12-handaudit-arena01.log`) puts player0's character world at
`(−103.728, −522.716, 255.825)` — versus `(−70.003, 80.657, 13.495)` in
`small_club_01`. The hand geometry measures **identically in both**:
`bone_L-hand` at `(−21.749, 0.197, 44.274)`, `hands_naked` bbox x ±25.22,
z 37.27…45.84, both hand bones `INSIDE`. ⇒ The palette carries the **pose** and
is invariant to the **placement**, in both venues. (The `arena_01` z of 255.825
is also the drum-riser band X7/X9 measured, so placement itself is being
computed — it simply is not in the palette.)

⇒ ★★★ **The deviation is CONSTANT across the restored meshes and the
never-broken control meshes alike.** By X10's rule — *a cause constant across
the working and the broken arm is not the cause* — **this is not a hand defect**,

## 11. Retracted hypotheses, with evidence

1. ⛔ **X11's "the hands read as detached from the sleeves."** Retracted. They
   are attached (§2.3), and player0 has no sleeves (§2.4).
2. ⛔ **My own, and the strongest lead I was handed: "the outfit meshes stay
   bound to the static shared magnet, so the hands hang off foreign bones."**
   (`BandCharacter.cpp:722-729, 757-768` documents the magnet and that head/hand
   meshes are deliberately never rebound.) **Refuted by direct measurement
   before any code was changed:** every hand mesh reports `bone slots: N owned
   by this character, 0 FOREIGN, 0 null`. A specific, plausible, well-sourced
   hypothesis killed by one printed set.
3. ⛔ **My own: "all four members' hands skin to the same bbox centred on x≈0,
   therefore the hands are displaced."** Retracted by the comparative control in
   the same block — `head`, `eyes`, `hair`, `torso` and the never-broken
   `fingernails` do the same thing. It is a whole-figure property (§8). ★ This
   is X10's rule catching *me*, one step before I reported a wrong headline.
4. ⛔ **My own: `gap(bone → geometry bbox)` is a sufficient detachment test.**
   Retracted: under the 17-unit perturbation both hand bones still reported
   `0.000 (INSIDE)`, because the bbox simply grew to contain them. A bbox is a
   loose hull. The signal came from the bbox *extents* and the centroid, not the
   containment test. ⚠ Reported rather than quietly dropped — one of my three
   sub-metrics is weak.
5. ⛔ **`gesture/JointUtl.cpp` as authoritative for RB3 bone names.** Retracted:
   it spells `bone_L-foreArm.mesh`; the shipped RB3 skeletons spell it
   `bone_L-forearm.mesh`. Caught by the instrument contradicting itself on one
   line — printing `foreArm ABSENT` while naming `bone_L-hand`'s parent as
   `bone_L-forearm.mesh`. JointUtl is Kinect/gesture (DC3-era) code.
6. ⛔ **X11's "use `--cam-manual` for a close-up."** Retracted: it takes no
   argument and swaps the projection path; it is not framing (§2.3).
7. ⛔ **My own, published then corrected: the §6.1 guard-state table.** Its first
   form marked **every** scatter edge unguarded. Three were wrong:
   `Morph.cpp:268 → flow/FlowNode.cpp` **is** guarded (`:265`), and rows 4 and 6
   each have a **second, already-guarded** includer
   (`LightPreset.cpp:1488`, `CharLipSync.cpp:458`) that I missed entirely. Cause:
   the `grep` behind the table printed `#include` lines without their
   surrounding `#ifndef`, and I published the guard column without opening the
   files. ★ Exactly the failure this lane spent §3 documenting in someone else's
   instrument — *a probe that reports a field it never actually measured* —
   committed by me, in the same document. Corrected in §6.1 by reading the eight
