## 1. ★★★ Milestone 1a — the bill, derived rather than inherited

The charter's instruction was explicit: derive it or do not use it. So the
method was not to read anything — it was to **add the call and read the
linker**.

### 1.1 The measurement

`OutfitConfig::Init()` added to `RegisterMiloObjectFactories()`; `rb3-render`
relinked; distinct undefined symbols collected
(`x20-undef-symbols.txt`): **48**.

| class | count | what they are |
|---|---|---|
| `extern Symbol` globals | **36** | `primary_color`, `mats`, `seams`, `recompose`, … |
| `BandPatchMesh` members | **11** | ctor, copy-ctor, `operator=`, `Render`, `ReProject`, `PreRender`, `PostRender`, `Compress`, `ListDrawChildren`, `operator>>`, `PropSync` |
| `gRB3OutfitComposeActive` | **1** | |

⚠ **The `48` coincides numerically with the `48` in the inherited bill, and

### 1.2 ★★★ 36 of 48 were free, via a seam already in the tree

`ObjMacros.h` has two spellings of the `SYNC_PROP` / `HANDLE` families,
selected by `RB3_SYNCPROP_LOCAL_STATIC` (`:372`) and `RB3_HANDLE_LOCAL_STATIC`
(`:125`). The default `#else` arm compares `sym == primary_color` — against a
**namespace-scope `extern Symbol` global**.

★ **Those globals are declared ~7083 times across
`src/system/utl/Symbols{,2,3,4}.h` and DEFINED NOWHERE IN THE TREE**
(2068 + 1658 + 1683 + 1674; grep over `src/` + `native/` finds no definition).
The X360 build never links, so nothing there can see it. Natively they are
undefined references that survive **only because `--gc-sections` drops the
referencing section** — and they all come due at once the moment a TU's
property table becomes live.

The `LOCAL_STATIC` arms spell the identical comparison as a function-local
`static Symbol _ps("primary_color")`. Two compile definitions on one TU:

| step | undefined symbols |
|---|---|
| `OutfitConfig::Init()` referenced | **48** |
| `+ RB3_SYNCPROP_LOCAL_STATIC` | **16** |
| `+ RB3_HANDLE_LOCAL_STATIC` | **12** |
| `+ x20_bandpatchmesh_link.cpp` | **0 — links** |

⚠ **Scoped to one TU deliberately, and the scope is a known limit, not a
tidy-up.** Every other native TU carrying a property table sits in the same
condition and is one GC decision from the same failure. Widening it would
change codegen for ~700 native TUs and is its own lane's gate.

### 1.3 ⛔ X9's blocker note: premise checked, premise false

The in-tree note at `milo_object_factories.cpp:467` read that
`BandPatchMesh.cpp` "is NOT compiled standalone … and LightPreset.cpp is not in
rb3-render's source list".

**MEASURED: 125 BandPatchMesh symbols are already defined in rb3-render's
link**, emitted from `rndobj/TexBlender.cpp.o`.
`src/system/rndobj/TexBlender.cpp:383` scatter-includes `AmbientOcclusion.cpp`
**unconditionally**, and that chains
`AmbientOcclusion → PropKeys → rndobj/Utl → UIListDir → LightPreset →
BandPatchMesh`. X9 was looking at `src/system/math/Rot.cpp:431`, which carries
the same edge but under `#if !HX_NATIVE`.

★ `native/cmake/ScatterIncludes.cmake` is **correct** here and its per-target
derivation is doing exactly what its header claims. The stale conclusion came
from reading one includer and stopping.

**What was genuinely missing was never the TU.** It was the nine ordinary
members that `src/system/bandobj/BandPatchMesh.cpp` — a **191-line partial
port**, as its own header states — never wrote.

### 1.4 What was supplied, and what was NOT

`native/src/x20_bandpatchmesh_link.cpp`, native-only:

- ⛔ **`ReProject()` and `PreRender()` NOT ported.** They reach

**Measured every run: `ReProject hits=0, PreRender hits=0`.** No unported

unqualified by them.

## 3. ⛔ Milestone 1c — the acceptance test FAILS, and that is the finding

**Acceptance was: the skin materials resolve real textures instead of
`dummy_*`, and the frame shows it. They do not.**

```
skinmat 'torso_naked.mat'   mesh 'hands_naked.mesh'            diffuse=dummy_torso.tex
skinmat 'legs_skin.mat'     mesh 'tightdistressedpants_skin.2' diffuse=dummy_legs.tex
skinmat 'feet_socks_skin.mat' mesh 'saddleshoe_skin.2.mesh'    diffuse=dummy_feet.tex
skinmat 'head_naked.mat'    mesh 'head.mesh'                   diffuse=NULL
```

Identical in kind to X19's. ★ **A control that misses its prediction is a
finding.** X19's chain — *no registration → 40 failures → `SyncOutfitConfig`
never runs → `SetSkinTextures` never runs → placeholders* — is correct in every
link it names, but it is not closed: removing its first link did not move the
last one.

⚠ **And this retroactively weakens X19's "negative proof".**
`grep -c "could not find" == 0` was offered as proof that `SetSkinTextures`
never ran. That count is still 0 **after** registration. A zero over a
predicate that only fires on *failure* cannot distinguish "never ran" from "ran
and found everything" — the same shape as this ladder's four vacuous passes.
The census, not the warning count, is what carries the claim.

### 3.2 The next link, located precisely

`BandCharacter::SyncOutfitConfig` (`BandCharacter.cpp:1630`) is the only route
to `SetSkinTextures` (`:1663`, and it fires only when the config's name parses
to the Symbol `skin`). It has **exactly two callers** (grep over `src/` +
`native/`):

| # | caller | status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `BandCharacter::SetDeformation()` `:1735-1739`, over `unk620` | reached only from `SyncObjects()` `:1077` |
| 2 | `BandCharacter::OnPostMerge()` `:2868`, over `unk630` | a `post_merge` handler; the driver never merges a wardrobe |

★ **A named prediction that FAILED, recorded because it was named first.** I
predicted call site 1 was blocked at its `if (clip)` guard (`:1675`), since
`GetDeformClip` (`BandCharDesc.cpp:59`) returns 0 whenever the file-static
`gDeforms` is null. **Measured: `male=male female=female` — both clips are
present.** That guard is *not* the blocker, and the deform-path assets are
loaded. So the remaining question is narrower than I expected: whether
`SyncObjects()` runs on the band members at all, and whether `AddObject`
(`:1586`) has populated `unk620` by then.

### 3.3 ★★★ What `SetSkinTextures` would bind — and it is all present

This matters because it decides whether the remaining work is a call-path gap
or a missing render feature, and the two have very different costs.

`SetSkinTextures` (`OutfitConfig.cpp:473-575`) does **not** bind the authored
`<gender>_<part>_diff.tex` when a `skin.cfg` exists. In that branch (`:503-506`)
it binds **`<part>_skin_diffuse_output.tex`** — the compose pass's **render
target** — and falls back to the authored diffuse only when `cfg` is null
(`:509`). All four `skin.cfg` exist (§2), so the RT branch is the live one.

**MEASURED (`x20-texsupply.log`): 22 `*_diffuse_output` render targets exist in
the graph, including `torso_skin_diffuse_output.tex`,
`legs_skin_diffuse_output.tex` and `head_skin_diffuse_output.tex`** — exactly
the three that table needs — alongside 34 authored `*_diff.tex` and 9 `dummy_*`,
of 392 `RndTex`.

⇒ **Nothing is missing from the asset side. The remaining gap is the call
path.**

### 3.4 ⚠ What is NOT claimed, and it is load-bearing for the next lane

I did **not** measure whether those render targets have any **content**.
Filling `*_skin_diffuse_output.tex` is the OutfitConfig compose pass, which is
what `gRB3OutfitComposeActive` gates — and the definition of that flag lives in
`RB3Quad.cpp`, in the engine's **`rb3`** GPU backend, which **this build does
not link** (§1.4). So it is entirely possible that invoking `SyncOutfitConfig`
binds the right object and yields a **blank** skin rather than a correct one.
**Reaching `SetSkinTextures` and getting correct skin are two milestones, not
one, and this lane cannot tell you they are the same.**

## 10. Recommended X21 shape

1. ★★★ **Removing the first link of a chain does not test the chain.** X19
   named five links and proved the first was broken. Fixing it moved nothing.
   A chain argument is only as good as its **weakest unmeasured** link, and

   number nobody had produced; producing it cost a single relink, and 36 of the

3. ★★★ **A zero from a failure-only predicate is not a negative result.**
   `grep -c "could not find" == 0` reads like proof the function never ran; it
   is equally consistent with it running and succeeding. Prefer a census that
   states its own denominator. §3
4. ★★★ **Check what the walker follows, not just what it counts.** `ObjDirItr`
   does not follow `SubDirs()`. My census said 0 where the truth was 40. X19's
   lesson, re-earned at the level below the one it was learned at. §7
5. ★★ **Let the instrument refuse.** The only reason (4) was not a published
   finding is that the census prints `VACUOUS` over a zero denominator and says
   not to read it. Build that refusal in before you need it. §7
6. ★★ **A stale comment is more expensive than no comment.** X9's blocker note
   was precise, confident, and wrong, and it deferred five lanes. Re-check the
   premise of any note you are about to act on. §1.3
